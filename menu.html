About RNLI Mission Chief

Take command of a Royal National Lifeboat Institution (RNLI) station and save lives at sea!

This simulation features authentic RNLI lifeboat classes and realistic maritime rescue scenarios.

About the RNLI

The Royal National Lifeboat Institution is a charity that saves lives at sea. Founded in 1824, the RNLI provides a 24-hour search and rescue service around the coasts of the United Kingdom and Ireland.

This is a fan-made game and is not officially affiliated with the RNLI.

How to Play

Objective

Respond to maritime emergencies and save lives at sea while managing your fleet of RNLI lifeboats.

Getting Started

  • You begin with £10,000 in credits and one Atlantic 85 inshore lifeboat
  • Maritime emergencies will appear automatically
  • Watch the map for mission locations

Gameplay

  • Dispatch Lifeboats: Select an available lifeboat and dispatch it to rescue missions
  • Complete Rescues: Lifeboats automatically complete missions and return to station
  • Earn Credits: Successful rescues reward you with credits (£3,000-£10,000)
  • Expand Fleet: Use credits to purchase additional lifeboats
  • Prioritize: Urgent missions (marked 🚨) should be handled first

Strategy Tips

  • Fast inshore boats (Atlantic 85, D-Class) are ideal for nearby emergencies
  • All-weather boats can handle longer-range missions
  • Always keep at least one lifeboat available
  • Build up your fleet to handle multiple simultaneous emergencies
